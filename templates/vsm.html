How to build a VSM

1. Drag Process boxes onto the canvas for each step in your value stream.

2. Double-click any process to enter metrics: C/T (cycle time in seconds), C/O (changeover time), Uptime %, operators, and batch size.

3. Add Inventory triangles between steps to represent wait time (enter days of supply).

4. Connect steps with Push or Pull flow arrows.

5. Set your Takt Time below (available time / demand) to see where bottlenecks are.

6. Add Kaizen bursts to mark improvement opportunities.

Metrics update live as you build. Lead Time = total wait + process time. PCE = process time / lead time.
