# Focus Group: SVEND AI-Guided OpEx Identity

**Date:** 2026-05-03
**Technique:** Research-Grounded Focus Group
**Personas:**
- Maria Gutierrez — QM at tier-2 auto stamping (Champion)
- Dave Kowalski — CI Director at med device contract mfg (Skeptic)
- Tameka Jackson — FSQA Manager at snack co-packer (Adjacent)
- Ray Nguyen — CNC job shop owner (Constraint)

## Prompt Tested

"SVEND is an AI-guided system for implementing operational excellence in manufacturing. You describe your problem to an AI assistant. It helps decompose the problem, runs analytical tools (SPC, DOE, capability, FMEA, RCA), explains results in plain language. Everything traced and documented — every analysis, decision, rationale is auditable. Knowledge retained when people leave. Value in first session. Force multiplier for problem solvers. $200-500/month per site."

## Synthesis

### Consensus (4/4 agreement)
1. **Knowledge retention is the universal wound.** Every persona lived through it. This is real pain, not theoretical.
2. **Audit defense is the purchase trigger.** Not OpEx philosophy. Surviving a specific audit.
3. **"AI-guided operational excellence" landed with nobody.** 4/4 rejected the headline language.
4. **Demo with MY data or nothing.** Generic demos don't convert. Every persona demands their own data, their own problem.
5. **Free trial is table stakes.** Not a differentiator — a minimum requirement.
6. **Reference customer in MY vertical.** Generic testimonials don't work.
7. **Inspectors/operators aren't statisticians.** The system must work for people as they are.

### Divergence
- **Statistical depth vs. accessibility:** Dave wants non-normal Cpk to the decimal. Tameka abandons if it takes >30 minutes. Can't serve both with same onboarding.
- **Price perception:** Same price, four readings — compelling (Ray), suspicious (Dave), in range (Maria), requires justification story (Tameka).
- **AI trust:** Four distinct fears — accuracy (Dave), safety (Ray), auditability (Tameka), explainability (Maria).
- **Integration:** No common target — Excel, ETQ+Minitab, SafetyChain, Mitutoyo/MeasurLink.

### Strongest Objection
Dave's credibility-on-the-line. He's been burned 3x. If he recommends this and it fails, his professional standing takes the hit. The more expert the evaluator, the higher the personal risk. The skeptic's objection is structural.

### Unmet Needs Discovered
1. **Short-run SPC** — Ray surfaced acute gap. MeasurLink can't handle it. Concrete underserved niche.
2. **CA management from unstructured sources** — Tameka's CAs live in email/notebooks. Needs document assembly, not analysis tools.
3. **Dollar quantification** — Tameka ($180K overfill), Ray ($6-8K/mo scrap), Maria (COPQ). They want money, not metrics.
4. **Data portability / exit rights** — Ray wants cancel demo with full export. Not addressed in pitch.
